    While you wait for a craving to pass

    1. Delay. Promise yourself 10 minutes. Urges rise, peak and fall, and you don’t have to act on them.
    2. Change your space. Go outside, wash your face with cold water, or move to a room with other people.
    3. Ground yourself. Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
    4. Check HALT. Are you Hungry, Angry, Lonely or Tired? Meet that need first.
    5. Reach out. Text or call one of your contacts. You don’t need a perfect explanation.

    Stopping some substances suddenly (especially alcohol and sedatives) can be medically dangerous. Speak to a doctor before quitting on your own.
